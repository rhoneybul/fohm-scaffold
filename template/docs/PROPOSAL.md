## 4. The skeleton (what's handed over)

Deliberately lean, working, documented:

1. Onboarding and a level assessment: meet people where they are
2. A progression path: the next one to three things to work on
3. Clips and questions to the coach, answered from the dashboard
4. Check-ins: what they worked on and how it felt
5. A content library, reusing what the owner already has
6. The owner's dashboard: everyone, everything they do, what needs a reply

Out of scope for the skeleton: (say what, and why).

## 5. Roadmap

- **Phase 0, the skeleton:** the above, live on the web, iOS and Android.
- **Phase 1, monetise the existing audience:** Pro (Stripe) for the path and coach feedback; bring in the people who already pay.
- **Phase 2, deepen the coaching:** multi-week programmes, integrations, more content.
- **Phase 3, scale:** AI-assisted first-pass feedback (the human coach stays the premium tier), more coaches.

Monetise the people you already have before chasing the whole market.
