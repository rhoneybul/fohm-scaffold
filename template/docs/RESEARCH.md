## The incumbent to study

we differ (niche, locality, the personal relationship).

## The strategic insight

Why this category is defensible, and what not to over-invest in.

## Market sizing (directional)

Bottom-up from the owner's own audience first (community, email list, yearly
clients), then the wider market. Mark what's an estimate.

## Open questions for the owner

- How big is the existing audience (community, email list, clients a year)?
- What's the revenue split across what they sell now?
- How much of their own time can go into content and replies in the app?
- What's the ambition: a recurring add-on to the business, or a venture-scale app?
- Budget: any capital, or sweat-equity / revenue-share from zero?
- Do they own their content and brand cleanly, to license into the app?
