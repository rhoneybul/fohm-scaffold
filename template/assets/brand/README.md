# The owner's brand artwork

Put the owner's own logo, wordmark and colour sheet here (PNG or SVG, the
largest you have). The app's logo is traced from these files, never redrawn
by hand, and the colours in `src/lib/theme.ts` are picked from them. Ask your
agent: "Trace the logo in assets/brand into an SVG component and use its
colours in theme.ts; record the system in DESIGN.md."
