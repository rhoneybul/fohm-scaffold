# Product

Fill this in before writing features. Coding agents read it to make product
decisions; the more concrete, the better the work.

## Platform

Web (Expo / React Native Web on Vercel), used on phones and laptops in the browser.

## Users

- **Members (primary):** who they are, and the one job they come to do.
- **The owner:** who runs it, and what they need to keep up with.

## Product purpose

One paragraph: what changes for a member who uses this, and how you'll know it worked.

## Positioning

What makes this different from the obvious alternative.

## Operating context

Where and when people use it (on a phone between tasks? on a laptop at a desk?), and what that means for the screens.

## Capabilities and constraints

What's in, what's out, what's paid, what must never happen.
