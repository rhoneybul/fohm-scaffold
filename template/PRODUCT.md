# Product

Fill this in before writing features. Coding agents make dozens of small
product decisions per feature, and this is what they decide from. Record
decisions with who made them and when, e.g. "(owner, Mar 2026)".

## The thesis

One line: what this app does for people, and why this business is the one to
make it. (For a coaching business: "your coach in your pocket, between
sessions": the app productises what the business already sells.)

## Platform

Web, iOS and Android from one codebase. Touch-first on a phone: 44pt targets,
safe areas, nothing that needs hover.

## Users

- **Members (primary):** who they are, where they're starting from, and the one job they come to do.
- **The owner:** who runs it, what they already sell, and what they need to keep up with.

## Product Purpose

What changes for a member who uses this, and how you'll know it worked.

## Positioning

What this is not (the crowded categories next door), and the gap it fills.
The wedge is usually the relationship (the owner's method, brand and
community), not the software.

## Operating Context

Where and when people use it, and what that means for the screens.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The skeleton's scope, what's deliberately out of scope for now, what's paid,
and what must never happen.

## Brand Commitments

The brand is the owner's own (their logo, colours and voice; their artwork in
`assets/brand/`, never redrawn by hand). No emoji. Must not look
AI-generated or generic. The apps the owner likes the feel of, from
DESIGN.md's references.

## Evidence on Hand

What's real (the owner's bio, offering, content, audience numbers) and what's
placeholder. No testimonials, numbers or outcomes may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. **One thing to do now.** A member always knows the next step.
2. **Meet people where they are.** Start them at their level, not at zero.
3. **The coach is real.** AI can help between sessions; the human is one tap away and sees everything.
4. **Feel over fake numbers.** How it went beats vanity metrics.
