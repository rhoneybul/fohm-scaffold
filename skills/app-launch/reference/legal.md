# The privacy policy and terms

**Why.** The stores require a privacy policy, and people deserve to know what is kept.

## What the agent does

- Keeps `src/lib/legal.ts` true: `privacy()` names only the services switched on, and a new integration or a change to what's stored gets its line there in the same change.
- Shows the operator, registration and registered office address from `src/lib/brand.ts` ([business.md](business.md)).
- Writes Google Play's Data safety and Apple's App Privacy answers from it ([stores.md](stores.md)).

## What is the person's

- Read `src/lib/legal.ts` and have it checked for where the business operates: it is a starting point, not legal advice.

## How to check it

- `https://<domain>/privacy` and `/terms` load and name the operator; the person says it's been checked.
